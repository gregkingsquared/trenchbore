Privacy policy for Trenchbore

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Trenchbore is published by King Squared. This page explains what the game does with information. The short version: the game does not collect any.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or anything else about you.

Your progress

Your progress (upgrades, records, settings and doubloons) is stored only on your own device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes it. If device backup is switched on in your Android settings, Android may include the game's save in your own Google backup; that is between you and Google.

Purchases

The game offers optional purchases. These are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google tells the game only that a purchase was completed, so the game can give you what you bought. Google's handling of your purchase is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

To provide purchases, the game includes Google's Play Billing software, which communicates with the Google Play app on your device.

Internet access

The game plays offline. It does not contact any server of ours, because there is none.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Contact
